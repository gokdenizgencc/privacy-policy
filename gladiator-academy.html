Privacy Policy – Gladiator Academy: Rome

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Gladiator Academy: Rome (package com.gokdeniz.gladiatoracademy, the "Game"). It explains what information the Game handles and how.

Summary

The Game does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting services.

Information Stored on Your Device

Your saves and settings (game progress, language, sound and similar preferences) are stored only locally on your device. We cannot access them. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes them.

Local Multiplayer (Same Wi-Fi Duel)

The optional 1v1 duel connects two devices directly over your local network. To find the other device and play, the Game uses the network permissions (Internet, network state, Wi-Fi state and Wi-Fi multicast). Game moves are exchanged only between the two devices on that network and are never sent to us. The Game has no chat and no way to share text, images or audio between players.

In-App Purchases

Optional purchases (premium content and donations) are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google handles the transaction under the Google Privacy Policy; the Game only receives confirmation of which product you own.

Advertising and Analytics

The Game shows no ads, does not use the advertising ID and does not include analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.

Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13. Because the Game collects no personal data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
